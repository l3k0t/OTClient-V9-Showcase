What's changed

More than a new version.
A modernized foundation.

Technical improvements and systems integrated into OTCv8, including adaptations from OTClient Redemption where needed.

⚙️

C++23 Core

Full C++23 builds, compatibility fixes, modern CMake, and organized dependencies.

🌐

Profile-based protocols

ProtocolProfile, ProtocolRegistry, and capability controls for different Tibia generations.

🎨

Modern sprites

Compressed sprite sheets, catalogs, appearances, and rendering integration.

📖

Cyclopedia and challenges

Cyclopedia, Bestiary, Bosstiary, Character Inspection, Taskboard, and Soulseals.

Progression and combat

More systems

Reward Wall, modern Store, Quick Loot, Stash, Health Circle, Analyser, and adapted interfaces.

A major milestone

From DAT/SPR to
modern game assets.

V9 introduces a modern asset-loading path for newer Tibia formats while retaining the legacy path for older clients.

One codebase, two generations.

Rather than relying solely on classic files, the client can recognize modern graphics structures.

  • Sprite sheets and LZMA decompression
  • Catalog and protobuf appearances
  • Modern data and animation loading
  • Version-aware asset detection
  • Legacy compatibility remains a goal
